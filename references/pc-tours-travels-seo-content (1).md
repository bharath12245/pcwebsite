## SEO Notes

- **Primary local keyword targets:** "tour operator Bengaluru," "pilgrimage tour packages Bengaluru," "holiday packages from Bangalore," "temple yatra Bengaluru," "Gandhi Nagar travel agency"
- **NAP consistency:** Use the exact same Name / Address / Phone format across the website footer, Contact page, and Google Business Profile for local SEO consistency
- **Schema markup recommended:** `LocalBusiness` / `TravelAgency` schema on the homepage and Contact page, using the address and phone numbers above; `FAQPage` schema for the homepage FAQ block
- **Founding year honesty:** Copy is phrased as "founded in 2025, backed by 20+ years of experience" throughout — this is accurate and actually a stronger trust signal than an inflated founding date, since it explains the expertise without misrepresenting the company's age
- **Next step:** once individual tour packages are finalized (names, durations, prices), each should get its own page with unique meta title/description following the pattern in Sections 3–4
- **All CTAs route to WhatsApp:** every "Book Now," "Enquire," and "Talk to Us" button across the site links to a WhatsApp chat (wa.me link), pre-filled with a message naming the specific tour where possible. No email address is published on the site — WhatsApp and phone are the only contact channels shown
